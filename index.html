Saved monthly data

Actual vs plan: the balance you typed as this month's opening balance minus what last month's plan predicted. A minus figure is money spent that was not in the plan. It only appears when you typed the real balance.

Backup and restore

Your data is stored only in this browser on this device. If the browser data is cleared or the device is lost, it is gone. A backup file is a copy you keep somewhere safe, such as cloud storage or email to yourself. A backup contains all your salary and expense data, so do not share it.
